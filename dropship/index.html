    02 three tiny steps

    how it works.

    No apps to install, no accounts to forget, no files taking a detour through a data center.

    01

    open it everywhere

    Start the app on one computer. Scan the QR or open the link on your phone, tablet or other laptop — as long as you're on the same Wi-Fi.

    same network · no install
    02

    pick a device

    Everyone shows up as a cute little card. Choose a device, drop in your files, add a note if you're feeling chatty, and hit send.

    files · notes · hearts
    03

    accept & keep

    The other side gets a proper little "incoming transfer" window. They accept, save what they need, and that's it. No cloud touched.

    you stay in control

    the fine print: this is a local-network app, not a secure vault. Use it on Wi-Fi you trust, and don't send your tax returns over the café guest network.

    03 the readme

    A themed little love letter to LocalSend — the open-source, cross-platform AirDrop alternative — rebuilt as a browser drop zone.

    readme.txt READ-ONLY

    hello neighbor,

    this is KURIZU.DROPSHIP :3

    LocalSend is the app I wish every operating system shipped with: nearby file sharing that just works, with no login and no cloud. This is my version of that idea, wearing my portfolio's purple pixel pajamas.

    Start the tiny Node server, open the link on another device, and you'll see it appear in the room. Files stream straight to this computer, wait for the other side to accept, and then download with a click. Notes work too — sometimes you just need to send a link and a heart.
