hello world,

i'm kurizu :3

I'm a self-taught developer and designer from the Philippines. I make small, useful things with too much personality: desktop pets, pixel games, browser tools, extensions, and websites that feel like a place instead of a page.

Most of my work starts with “what if this existed?” and ends with a purple UI, a custom cursor, and me learning three new things at 2am. I care about the details you feel more than the ones you see — smooth motion, tiny sounds, and software that's nice to spend time in.
